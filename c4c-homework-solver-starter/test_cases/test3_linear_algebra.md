# Linear Algebra Worksheet 1: Matrices and Determinants

## Questions

1. What does it mean for a square matrix to be invertible? State a condition on the determinant.

2. What is an eigenvalue of a matrix $A$? Write the defining equation.

## Problems

1. Compute the determinant of the matrix

$$\begin{vmatrix}1 & 2\\3 & 4\end{vmatrix}$$

2. Find the inverse of the matrix

$$\begin{bmatrix}2 & 1\\1 & 3\end{bmatrix}$$

3. Find the eigenvalues and corresponding eigenvectors of the matrix

$$\begin{bmatrix}2 & 1\\1 & 2\end{bmatrix}$$

4. Solve the linear system $A\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{b}$ where

$$A = \begin{bmatrix}2 & 1\\1 & 3\end{bmatrix},\qquad \mathbf{b} = \begin{bmatrix}5\\7\end{bmatrix}$$

5. Compute the dot product of the vectors

$$\mathbf{u} = \langle 1, 2, 3 \rangle,\qquad \mathbf{v} = \langle 4, 5, 6 \rangle$$

6. Compute the cross product of the vectors

$$\mathbf{u} = \langle 1, 0, 0 \rangle,\qquad \mathbf{v} = \langle 0, 1, 0 \rangle$$

7. Find the transpose of the matrix

$$\begin{bmatrix}1 & 2 & 3\\4 & 5 & 6\end{bmatrix}$$

8. Multiply the matrices

$$\begin{bmatrix}1 & 2\\3 & 4\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}5 & 6\\7 & 8\end{bmatrix}$$

9. Find the rank of the matrix

$$\begin{bmatrix}1 & 2\\2 & 4\end{bmatrix}$$

10. Find the trace of the matrix

$$\begin{bmatrix}2 & 1\\1 & 3\end{bmatrix}$$
